# Age + shape-stage conditional diffusion pilot

- Real training windows: **174**
- Independent experiment IDs used: **B2, C1, C2**
- Candidate synthetic windows: **256**
- Accepted after grouped quality gate: **121** (47.3%)
- Device: **cpu**

Stage labels are coarse temperature-shape labels (`pre_rapid`, `rapid_peak`, `post_peak`), not venting ground truth.

Accepted windows are training augmentation only and must not be counted as additional independent experiments.
